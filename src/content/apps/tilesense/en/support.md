## Where should I start?

Start with Mahjong Basics on the lessons tab, then work through tile efficiency, defense, push-or-fold and calls. Visual explanations help you understand missed answers, and the practice tab gives you a place to review.

Today's practice has 5 questions. The skill check has 10 discard questions and charts your progress. Focused drills cover discards, shanten and effective tiles. Missed questions are saved in the mistake book and removed when you answer them correctly in review.

## What's free, and what does the unlock include?

Free content includes the Basics unit, the first two Tile Efficiency lessons, today's practice and the skill check once each per day, and 5 hand analyses a day.

The full unlock is a one-time purchase, with no subscription. It includes all lessons, focused drills, mistake review and unlimited analysis. Apple shows the current price in the app.

## How do I restore a purchase?

Use the same Apple Account you bought it with, then open **Me → Restore purchases**, or tap **Restore purchases** on the unlock screen. This may require a connection so Apple can verify your purchase.

Restoring a purchase restores access to unlocked content, not learning progress. If it fails, check your Apple Account and connection, then email us the error message. Refund requests can be submitted through [Apple's refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).

## Does my progress sync between devices?

Learning progress and settings stay on the current device, without cross-device sync. Using Delete App in iOS removes this local data; Offload App keeps documents and data.

## How do I change the language or tile style?

**Me → Language** opens TileSense's page in iOS Settings. If there is no Language option, add a second language in Settings → General → Language & Region first. TileSense supports English, Simplified Chinese and Japanese.

Open **Me → Tile style** to choose classic artwork or large numbers, with three tile-back colors.

## What does the analyzer support?

The analyzer supports 13-tile and Taiwanese 16-tile hands, comparing shanten and effective tiles for each discard. You can mark tiles already seen on the table to account for the draws that remain.

Enable or disable special hands, including seven pairs and thirteen orphans, in **Me → Rules for the analyzer**. Results depend on the selected rules, your hand and seen tiles. Include these details when asking about a result.
