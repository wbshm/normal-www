## Courses

7 units and 34 lessons cover tiles, chow, pung, kong, shanten, effective tiles, partial melds, defense, push-or-fold and calls. Lessons include questions and visual explanations.

Courses also cover rule differences: seven pairs, thirteen orphans, Sichuan's void suit and tile swap, and the Guobiao minimum score.

## Practice

- **Today's practice**: 5 questions, about 2 minutes.
- **Skill check**: 10 discard questions with a score history.
- **Focused drills**: practice choosing a discard, counting shanten or finding effective tiles.
- **Mistake book**: missed questions are saved automatically and removed when you answer them correctly in review.

Answers are checked by a tile-efficiency engine and include visual explanations. Daily reminders are off by default. Choose a reminder time; it skips days when you have already completed the practice.

## Hand analyzer

Enter a hand and compare the shanten and effective tiles for each possible discard. Mark tiles you have already seen on the table to compare the draws that are still available.

The analyzer supports **13-tile and Taiwanese 16-tile hands**. Optional special hands, including seven pairs and thirteen orphans, can be enabled in the analyzer's rule settings.

## Free content and full version

Free content includes the Basics unit, the first two Tile Efficiency lessons, today's practice and the skill check once each per day, and 5 hand analyses a day.

A single in-app purchase unlocks all lessons, focused drills, mistake review and unlimited hand analysis. There is no subscription; the price is shown by Apple in the app. Use the same Apple Account to restore your purchase after reinstalling or changing phones.

## Device support and data

No account is needed. Learning progress and settings stay on your device, without cross-device progress sync. Purchases and restoring purchases use Apple's App Store services and may require a connection.

TileSense is made for iPhone, requires iOS 17 or later, and supports English, Simplified Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian and Malay. Choose classic tile artwork or large numbers, with three tile-back colors.

TileSense is for lessons and exercises only, with no matches, gambling, chips or real-money play.
