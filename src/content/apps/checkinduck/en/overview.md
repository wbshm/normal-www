## Automatic check-ins

Choose an app and set a daily usage target in minutes, a deadline and a repeat rule. The task checks off automatically when you reach the target. Manual check-ins are also available.

Automatic check-ins require Apple Screen Time / Family Controls authorization. They measure usage time, not what you read, learn or complete inside another app.

## Records and reminders

- **Today's tasks:** filter by completion status.
- **Deadline reminders:** get a notification before a task is due.
- **Calendar and notes:** review completed, pending and missed days, and add notes.
- **Home Screen widgets:** see today's check-in status.

## Premium

Premium unlocks unlimited tasks, full history beyond the free window and custom reminder lead times. Apple handles purchases and restores. Options, prices and terms are shown on the in-app purchase screen.

## Permissions

CheckInDuck monitors only the apps you select and authorize. It does not read their messages, typed text, taps or screen content. Change a task or revoke authorization to stop monitoring it.
