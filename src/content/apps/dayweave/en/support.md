## How do I choose my federal state?

Open **Settings → State**. If your state has optional local holidays, you can enable them there. The bundled school holidays cover 2026 to 2028. You can check official dates through the [Standing Conference of the Ministers of Education and Cultural Affairs](https://www.kmk.org/service/ferienregelung.html).

## How is my remaining leave calculated?

Dayweave deducts requested and approved leave and shows planned days separately. Available carry-over days are used first until the expiry date you set. In Settings, check your yearly entitlement, carry-over, workdays and rules for 24 and 31 December.

## How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your iPhone Home Screen, add a widget and choose **Dayweave**. The small widget counts down to your next leave or public holiday. Pro also includes a medium widget with your remaining leave balance.

## How do I restore Dayweave Pro?

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy Pro, then choose **Restore Purchases** in **Settings** or on the Pro purchase screen. Apple needs an internet connection to check the purchase. Restoring Pro restores access to its features. Your leave records stay on the device where you entered them.
