This policy applies to the Dayweave iPhone app from **啵啵的猫粮计划**.

## Data stored on your device

Dayweave stores your selected region, work schedule, leave settings, leave entries and blackout dates on your device in a local storage area shared with its widgets. The app works without an account. It does not send these records to us or sync them across devices, and it contains no ads, tracking tools or analytics SDKs.

## Reminders and calendar export

If you turn on carry-over reminders, Dayweave asks for notification permission and schedules the reminders on your device. You can change that permission in iOS Settings. When you export leave, Dayweave creates an ICS file and opens the iOS share sheet so you can choose where to send it. This does not require access to your calendar.

## Purchases through Apple

Apple handles Dayweave Pro purchases and restores through the App Store. Dayweave checks the purchase status through StoreKit and caches it on your device for offline use by the app and its widgets. We do not receive your payment details. Apple handles purchase data under its [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Deletion and backups

Deleting the app removes its local leave records from your device. An iOS backup may contain a copy, depending on your backup settings. We cannot access or restore your on-device records remotely. Apple manages App Store purchase records separately.

## Support and privacy contact
