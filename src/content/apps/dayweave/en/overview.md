## Find bridge days

Use a few days of annual leave to join up weekends and public holidays. Dayweave suggests longer breaks based on your German federal state, working days, leave budget and dates you’ve already entered. You can save a suggestion as planned leave.

## See public and school holidays together

The calendar shows public holidays for all 16 German states, school holidays from 2026 to 2028, ISO week numbers and your leave. Depending on your state, you can enable additional local holidays in Settings. Tap a day to see details or add leave.

## See how your leave balance is calculated

Planned leave is shown separately. Entries count against your balance when you mark them as requested or approved. You can review deductions day by day, set an expiry date for days carried over and choose whether 24 and 31 December cost 0, 0.5 or 1 leave day each.

## Free features and Dayweave Pro

Bridge-day suggestions for this year and next, the calendar, manual leave tracking and the countdown widget are free.

Dayweave Pro is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing. It adds custom budgets and planning goals, school holiday filters, blackout dates, a full annual plan and planning for the year after next. Pro also includes custom workdays, reminders before carried-over leave expires, ICS export and a leave balance widget.

Dayweave runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later and supports English and German. Planning, the calendar and leave tracking work offline without an account. Purchasing or restoring Pro requires an internet connection.
