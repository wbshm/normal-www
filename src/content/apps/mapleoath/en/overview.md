## Questions and courses

538 questions are based on Discover Canada. Each cites a chapter and section of the official guide and links to its source.

Five learning paths contain 56 stops covering citizenship, history, government, symbols and culture, and Canada's provinces and territories. Each stop includes a short lesson and questions.

Chapter practice follows the guide in sets of about ten questions. Progress is saved, and questions already answered correctly are skipped.

## English and Chinese

English questions include optional Chinese translations and read-aloud audio. The interface supports English and Simplified Chinese. The actual citizenship test is offered in English or French.

## Practice and mock tests

- **Mock tests:** 20 questions, 45 minutes and a passing score of 15, with answer review after submission.
- **Mistake review:** missed questions return on a spaced-repetition schedule.
- **Province questions:** practise facts about your selected province or territory, including its capital and region.
- **Dated facts:** information about officials includes the date checked and official sources. Check for updates before your test.

## Free content and full version

Free content includes the first 3 chapters, the whole “Becoming a citizen” path, the first stops of the other paths and 1 full mock test.

A one-time purchase unlocks all chapters, province questions, learning paths and unlimited mock tests. No subscription. Family Sharing is supported, and Apple shows the price in the app.

No account or ads. Study content works offline, and your progress stays on your device. Purchases, restoring purchases and opening official websites need an internet connection.

Requires an iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

MapleOath is not affiliated with the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). The official study guide, [Discover Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada.html), is available free from IRCC.
