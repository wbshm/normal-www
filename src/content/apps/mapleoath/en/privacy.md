MapleOath (枫誓) is an app for preparing for the Canadian citizenship test. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## We do not collect your data

- There is no account and no sign-in.
- The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
- The app does not send your answers, progress or settings to us or to anyone else.

## What is stored on your device

Your language, province, test date, practice history, mistakes and mock test results are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether the full version has been purchased.

## Links to other websites

The app links to official websites such as canada.ca and elections.ca. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
