## Where should I start?

Open Study and choose a guided path. Each stop starts with a short introduction, followed by questions. You can also practise by chapter in sets of about ten questions; your progress is saved so you can continue later.

Practice includes mistake review with spaced repetition. The Mock Test tab lets you try a timed test and review the result afterwards.

## How do I change the language, province or test date?

Open **Me → Language** to choose English or Simplified Chinese. In the same section, select your province or territory and turn on **I know my test date** to set a date.

You can show or hide Chinese translations while practising. Chinese is off by default in mock tests. You can also choose to include up to 2 province questions in each mock test from Me.

## What's free, and what does the full version include?

Free content includes the first 3 chapters, the whole “Becoming a citizen” path, the first stops of the other paths and 1 full mock test. The free mock test is used when you start it; leaving early does not restore it.

The full version is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks all chapters, province questions, all guided paths and unlimited mock tests, with Family Sharing. Apple shows the price in the app.

## How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy the full version, then open **Me → Restore purchase**, or select **Restore purchase** on the unlock screen. An internet connection is needed for Apple to check the purchase.

If no purchase is found, check your Apple Account and connection, then try again. If the issue continues, email us with the message shown. Restoring a purchase restores access to the full version; it does not restore your study progress.

## Can I study offline? Does my progress sync?

Study content is included in the app and works offline. Purchases, restoring purchases and opening official source websites need an internet connection.

Your settings, practice history, mistakes and mock test results stay on the current device. The app does not sync study progress between devices. Deleting the app deletes its local data.

## How do I check information that changes?

Open **Me → Up-to-date facts** to see recorded information, the date it was checked and official source links. Information about leaders can change; check the source for your test day. Changing your province also changes the relevant province-specific information.

If you spot an outdated item, send us the item name and official source link so we can review it.

## Is MapleOath an official government app?

No. MapleOath is not affiliated with the Government of Canada or Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Its questions are study exercises based on Discover Canada, not a copy of the actual test.

The official [Discover Canada guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/corporate/publications-manuals/discover-canada.html) is available free. For information about your actual test, use the [official test page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-citizenship/test.html) and your IRCC invitation.
