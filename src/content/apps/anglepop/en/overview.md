## How to play

Choose a starting point, pull back to aim and release. Bounce the ball through every block in one shot to finish the level. Missing a block resets the puzzle. Retries are unlimited.

## Levels and features

- **Main levels:** 288 puzzles across three difficulty groups.
- **Daily challenge:** 75 built-in puzzles, rotating at midnight in Beijing time (UTC+8).
- **Reflection preview and magnifier:** inspect the route and zoom in on details.
- **Hint cards:** claim one free card each day from your inventory. It marks the first block on a suggested route.
- **Settings:** light and dark themes, with separate music, sound and vibration controls.

## Purchases and device support

History Trajectories is a one-time in-app purchase. It shows the most recent failed route for a level during the current app session. Routes are not saved permanently.

Puzzles are built in. No account is needed, and progress and settings stay on your device. Purchases and restores are handled by Apple and may require an internet connection.

Anglepop is made for iPhone, requires iOS 17 or later, and includes English and Simplified Chinese interfaces.
