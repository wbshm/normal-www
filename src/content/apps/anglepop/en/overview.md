## A little geometry. A lot of possibility.

Choose your starting point, pull back to aim, and let go. Your ball follows the reflections you planned. Clear every block in a single shot to finish the puzzle.

Missed a block? The whole puzzle resets, ready for another idea. There is no limit on retries. Take your time, experiment, and enjoy that moment when everything clicks.

## Small puzzles, plenty to explore

- **288 main levels** across three difficulty groups, from gentle beginnings to real head-scratchers.
- **A daily challenge** drawn from 75 built-in puzzles, rotating at midnight in Beijing time (UTC+8).
- **A true reflection preview** to help you plan, plus an optional magnifier for a closer look.
- **Light and dark themes**, with separate music, sound, and vibration controls.
- **Hints when you want them.** Claim one free hint card each day from your inventory. A hint marks the first block on a suggested route.

## Your own pace, on your own device

The puzzles are built into the app. Play without creating an account; your progress and preferences stay on your device. Purchases and restoring purchases use Apple's App Store services and may require a connection.

History Trails is an optional, one-time in-app purchase. It lets you see the most recent failed route for a level during the current app session. It unlocks a feature, rather than creating a permanent archive of every shot.

Anglepop is made for iPhone, requires iOS 17 or later, and includes English and Simplified Chinese interfaces.
