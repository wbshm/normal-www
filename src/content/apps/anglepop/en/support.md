## Get in touch

For a technical issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Anglepop version, and what happened. A level number and a screenshot can help when relevant. You can send feedback in English or Chinese.

## How do I play?

1. Press an empty area of the board to choose a starting point.
2. Drag **opposite to the direction you want to launch**. The preview shows the planned reflection route. Pulling farther changes the preview length, not the ball's speed.
3. Release to launch. Clear every block with that one shot to complete the level.

If the shot fails, the level resets completely. Retry as often as you like. You can enable the magnifier in Settings for precise aiming.

## How do hints work?

Open your hint inventory to claim one free card each day. A hint uses one card and marks the first block on a suggested route. It does not show a complete solution. Reopening an already-used hint for that level during the same app session does not spend another card.

Free claims reset at midnight Beijing time (UTC+8). Unclaimed cards are not added automatically. Paid hint packs are not currently available.

## When does the daily challenge change?

At **00:00 Beijing time (UTC+8)**. The daily challenge rotates through 75 puzzles bundled with the app; it does not download a new level each day. Everyone on the same app version sees the same puzzle for a given Beijing date.

Your attempts for that day's challenge are saved locally. Once you complete it, that day's result is locked. Main-level progress and daily results are separate.

## Where is my progress saved?

Main-level progress, daily results, hint inventory, and settings are saved on your iPhone. Anglepop does not provide its own account system, cloud sync, or progress transfer between devices or the WeChat version.

Deleting the app may remove your local progress. A device backup, if available, is managed by iOS and your backup settings. Restoring a purchase does not restore completed levels or hint inventory.

## What does History Trajectories unlock?

History Trajectories is an optional **one-time purchase**, not a subscription. It lets you display the most recent failed route and collision points for a level during the current app session. The trail is not a permanent archive and is cleared when the app session ends.

Open the locked History Trajectories entry in Settings to see the feature explanation, local App Store price, purchase button, and **Restore Purchases**. Once unlocked, you can switch its display on or off in Settings.

## How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account that originally bought History Trajectories, then open its unlock page and select **Restore Purchases**. An internet connection may be required. Apple verifies the purchase; a refunded or revoked purchase no longer unlocks the feature.

If restoration fails, check your connection and Apple Account, then contact us with the error message. Requests for App Store refunds can be submitted through [Apple's refund service](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/).
