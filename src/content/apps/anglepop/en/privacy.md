## About this policy

This policy applies to the Anglepop iPhone app provided by **Normal Apps**, an independent developer. It explains the app's current data practices and how we handle information you choose to send for support.

For privacy questions or requests, contact **[wrq142857@gmail.com](mailto:wrq142857@gmail.com?subject=Anglepop%20privacy)**.

## Data stored on your device

Anglepop stores game progress and preferences locally on your device using iOS app storage. This includes:

- Completed main-level identifiers.
- The current daily challenge's date, level, attempts, and completion status.
- Hint-card inventory and the date of the last free claim.
- Music, sound, vibration, theme, magnifier, and History Trajectories display preferences.

The most recent failed route and collision points for a level are kept in memory during the current app session. They are not a permanent history archive.

Anglepop does not transmit this game data to a developer-operated server. The app has no developer account system or app-provided cloud sync. Device backups may include app data according to your iOS and backup settings; those services are managed by Apple or your chosen backup method.

## Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current version of Anglepop does not include advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal information.

## Purchases handled by Apple

Optional in-app purchases and purchase restoration use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. Anglepop reads verified purchase entitlements on your device to determine whether a paid feature is unlocked. These services may require a network connection.

Apple processes payments. Normal Apps does not receive your payment-card details. Apple's purchase records, payment processing, and related data are governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Deleting Anglepop does not delete purchase records held by Apple.

## Information you send for support

If you email us, we receive your email address, the message, and any information or attachments you choose to include, such as your device model, app version, or a screenshot. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and provide necessary follow-up.

Support email is handled through Gmail, Google's email service, subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not use support correspondence for advertising or sell it. We retain it only as needed for support, follow-up, and applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by contacting us; information that must be retained for legal obligations will be retained only for that purpose.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain in the app's storage until removed by you or the operating system. You can remove the app's on-device data by deleting the app, rather than offloading it. Backup copies are controlled by your backup settings; deleting the app does not necessarily delete earlier backups. Session-only failed routes are discarded when the app session ends.

Normal Apps cannot access, recover, or remotely delete your local game progress. For support correspondence held by us, you may request access, correction, or deletion through the contact address above, subject to applicable requirements. Apple manages its own purchase records and privacy requests.

## Children

Anglepop does not ask players to submit personal information inside the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us in a support email, contact us so we can address it and delete information where appropriate.

## Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising scripts, or a contact form. GitHub may process technical request information, including visitors' IP addresses, for hosting and security under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Website hosting data is separate from Anglepop's local game data.
